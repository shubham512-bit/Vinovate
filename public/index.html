A small robot. A big mission.

WALL-E can’t see the hidden map. He explores one square at a time, listens for EVE’s signal, and updates his belief from noisy direction and strength readings.

  • Run lets the planner choose the next safe action.
  • Step +1 advances one decision so you can inspect the reasoning.
